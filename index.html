How to apply these in Free Fire
  1. Open Free Fire → tap the gear icon → Sensitivity tab.
  2. Drag each slider (General, Red Dot, 2x, 4x, Sniper, Free Look) until the number matches what's shown here.
  3. DPI and Button Size are further down the same settings screen.
  4. Save, then jump into a custom room to feel it out before you queue ranked.
  5. OB55 note: the new M7, RPK, and Hawk use an active recoil model — you drag to counter it instead of relying on spread. Try the Pro or Glide preset with these for smoother manual control.
